## Tool kits

With a basic kit, you avoid the −5 for working without the right tools. Better kits also give a bonus.

| Kit                                   | Basic  | Professional (+1) | Top of the line (+2) |
| ------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------------- | -------------------- |
| Toolkit (Engineering)                 | $2,500 | $15,000           | $60,000              |
| Lockpicks and bypass tools (Security) | $1,500 | $12,000           | $50,000              |
| Medkit (Medicine)                     | $1,000 | $10,000           | $40,000              |
| Lab kit (Science)                     | $4,000 | $20,000           | $80,000              |

**Medkit supplies:** a medkit holds **6 uses**. Each heal or stabilization uses one. Refilling it costs **$1,500**, whatever the grade. An empty medkit still counts as the right tools for other Medicine work, like installing chrome.

A full surgical suite isn't a kit you carry. It's a room you rent or own.

## Brands

Who made something changes how it works. A brand gives a **trait** and changes the price; multiply the price above by the brand's. A brand never gives a weapon a bonus to hit. (The foreign military maker's Smart trait is a built-in smart-link, which is a mod.) An item with no brand is an ordinary make: no trait, ×1.

The makers below are types, not names. Give each one a name that fits your setting.

### Weapons

| Maker                                           | Trait                                                                                                                           | Price |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Utility maker                                   | **Rugged:** the Jam token can't target it, and it only loses ammo on a failure                                                  | ×1    |
| Heavy-caliber maker (sold under the counter)    | **Heavy:** damage die one size bigger (to a maximum of d12), 1 less ammo (minimum 1). Pistols, rifles and heavy weapons         | ×1.5  |
| Foreign military maker (sold under the counter) | **Smart:** built-in smart-link, +2 with the implant. Checkpoint scanners flag it. Pistols, carbines and grenade launchers       | ×1.5  |
| Foreign state arsenal (sold through smugglers)  | **Long:** effective range and reach one band farther, like a built-in scope (doesn't stack with one). Pistols and rifles        | ×1.25 |
| Back-room print                                 | **Clean:** scanners miss it. **Flimsy:** it breaks on a failure. Holdouts, pistols and SMGs only. No brand and no serial number | ×0.5  |

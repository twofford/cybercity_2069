# Character Creation

1. **Stats:** assign **3, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0** to Brawn, Charm, Intelligence, Nerve, Reflexes and Tech.
2. **Background:** pick one, or make your own. It gives one skill at 2 and four at 1.
3. **Free skills:** add **6 more points** anywhere. No skill can start above **3**.
4. **Gear:** take your background's kit, **or** $45,000 to spend (see [[Equipment]]). Nobody starts with chrome, but you can buy it with starting money (see [[Chrome]]).
5. **Work out:** HP = 10 + (2 × Brawn). Bulk limit = 6 + (2 × Brawn). Flesh starts at 10, minus any chrome. XP starts at 0 and Rep at Nobody (see [[Advancement]]).
6. **Personal details:** a Drive, a Line, a Tie and a Debt (examples below). They tell the GM what to push on.

See [[Rules Reference]] for what everything means, and use the blank [[Character Sheet]].

## Backgrounds

A background is who the character was **before** the crew. Every kit, gear plus cash, is worth **$45,000**.

**Make your own** if none of these fit: a name, one skill at 2 and four at 1, and $45,000 of gear and cash.

## Drive

What do they want badly enough to risk their life for?

1. Get out of the slums and into the rich part of the city.
2. Pay off the debt before it pays them a visit.
3. Find whoever killed someone they loved.
4. Buy their family's way out of the tenements.
5. Become someone the whole city knows.
6. Get back the body parts that were repossessed.
7. Bring down one specific corp.
8. Protect their neighborhood from whatever's coming.
9. Find out the truth about the disaster that took everything from them.
10. Make enough to never have to do this again.

## Line

What won't they do, whatever the money?

1. Hurt a kid.
2. Work for the corp that ruined their life.
3. Kill someone who isn't trying to kill them.
4. Sell out a crewmate.
5. Take chrome off someone who's still alive.
6. Sell drugs.
7. Leave someone behind.
8. Lie to their family.
9. Take charity from a corp.
10. Go back to the place where it all went wrong.

## Tie

Who's the one person in their life?

1. A younger sibling still living in the tenements.
2. An old commanding officer who drinks too much.
3. A ripperdoc who patched them up for free once.
4. An ex who works for a corp now.
5. A grandparent who remembers the city before it went bad.
6. A kid they're informally raising.
7. A fence who always gives them a fair price.
8. A former partner, now in a different crew.
9. A priest, rabbi or imam who never gives up on them.
10. A mentor who taught them everything, and then vanished.

## Debt

Who do they owe, and what?

1. A chrome maker: their cybereyes are still company property, and the company can call in a favor whenever it likes.
2. A fixer: a job went wrong on their watch, and the fixer lost a client over it.
3. A repo gang: the gang let them keep a kidney, for now, in exchange for help whenever the gang asks.
4. A ripperdoc: saved their life after a bad night, and has never let them forget it.
5. A loan shark: the loan was paid back years ago, but the shark says that they still owe, and decides what that means.
6. A gang boss: a favor, to be named later.
7. A crewmate: their life.
8. A corp: they walked out on a contract, and the corp wants them back to finish it.
9. Family back home: gave up everything to get them out, and expects them to make it count.
10. Someone they don't know: the debt was sold, and they don't know who holds it now or what that person will ask for.
